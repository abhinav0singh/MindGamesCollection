Each node is one sticker, coloured by the face it started on, and each face is a fan of nine nodes sitting on three neighbouring rings. The three ring families are the three axes of the cube. Turning a layer moves 20 stickers (12 for a middle slice); the nodes glide around that axis's rings while the cube turns by the same angle. You control the angle: drag slowly and watch both, let go past about 30° to complete the turn, or stop short and it springs back. “Order” is how many times you'd repeat the current scramble as a move sequence before the cube is solved again. Auto-solve plays your moves back in reverse.
